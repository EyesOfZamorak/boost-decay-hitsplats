# Boost Decay Hitsplats

Shows a small hitsplat over your character each time overheal Hitpoints decay by 1.

Food and potions such as anglerfish and Saradomin brews can raise your Hitpoints above
their normal level. The game then lowers them one point at a time back to normal, and
normally that only shows as a quiet change on the HP orb. This plugin puts a distinct
splat on your character for each point, so you can see the overheal wearing off.

The splat never appears for real damage, poison, venom, healing, level-ups, logins or
world hops, and its look is clearly different from game hitsplats.

## Options

- **Show number**: draw the "1" on the splat
- **Duration**: how long each splat stays on screen
- **Vertical offset**: move the splat up or down
- **Preview**: keep showing sample splats on your character while you adjust the look
- **Hitpoints**: turn the splat on or off, pick the Hitpoints skill icon or a drawn
  circle, and choose the circle's color
